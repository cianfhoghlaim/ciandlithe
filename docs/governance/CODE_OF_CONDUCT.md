# CIANDLITHE — Code of Conduct

> **Per the cianchosaint `governance/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` precedent** — the canonical code of conduct for the ciandlithe project.

## §1 — Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the ciandlithe project a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of:
- Age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression
- Level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion
- Sexual identity and orientation, or any other personal attribute

## §2 — Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:
- The use of sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information (such as a physical or electronic address) without explicit permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## §3 — Specific to Ciandlithe

The ciandlithe project deals with civil-litigation preparation. **Additional standards apply**:

1. **The Person-of-Interest clause (`LICENSE.md §5.2`)** — Do NOT surface the names of non-public individuals in any shared view, PR comment, or issue tracker without analyst review.
2. **The no-auto-submit constraint (`LICENSE.md §3.8`)** — Do NOT propose code changes that directly submit forms to courts.ie / irishstatutebook.ie / nidirect.gov.uk / scotcourts.gov.uk / judiciary.uk.
3. **The OSINT ceiling (`LICENSE.md §5.1`)** — Do NOT propose DLT sources for URLs not on the OSINT allowlist.
4. **Solicitor-client privilege** — Do NOT include real case-study evidence (audio, WhatsApp exports, screenshots, medical records) in PRs. Use only the leabharlann PDF references (read-only context).

## §4 — Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported by contacting the project team at [INSERT EMAIL]. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response that is deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances. The project team is obligated to maintain confidentiality with regard to the reporter of an incident. Further details of specific enforcement policies may be posted separately.

Project maintainers who do not follow or enforce the Code of Conduct in good faith may face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by other members of the project's leadership.
